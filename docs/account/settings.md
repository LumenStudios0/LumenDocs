# Account settings

## Discord sign-in

Your Lumen Studios account is linked through Discord. The current sign-in lasts for up to one hour and is stored in this browser tab only.

To sign out, open your account menu in the top navigation and select **Sign out**. Account profile editing and notification preferences will be available when account settings are connected to the customer service.

## Privacy

License and purchase details are not connected to this dashboard yet. The license IP preview saves data only in this browser and does not update a server-side license.
